Flickr-Faces-HQ (FFHQ) attribution

This non-commercial educational visualization uses a deterministic sample of
FFHQ aligned 128 × 128 thumbnails to derive an average face and PCA basis.
The source images were converted to grayscale and the displayed artifacts are
derived reconstructions and principal components.

Dataset: https://github.com/NVlabs/ffhq-dataset

Citation:
Tero Karras, Samuli Laine, and Timo Aila. “A Style-Based Generator
Architecture for Generative Adversarial Networks.” CVPR 2019.

FFHQ is distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Individual source photographs
retain their original licenses and attribution requirements. The complete
selected-record provenance manifest is produced by the offline build pipeline
and retained outside the public web bundle.
